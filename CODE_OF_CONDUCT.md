# Code of conduct

Cryonis is an open-source project where contributors should be able to participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give actionable review feedback and accept it professionally.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses and project decisions.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a contribution or review.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal insults, doxxing, deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith argument, publishing private communications, or retaliation against a reporter are not acceptable in project spaces.

Project spaces include the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, community calls and public communication made on behalf of Cryonis.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. If the report concerns one maintainer, contact another maintainer when possible.

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and impartially as practical. They may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, reject contributions, or permanently ban participation. A maintainer named in a report must not make the final enforcement decision.

False reports made knowingly and in bad faith may also result in enforcement. Good-faith reports that cannot be substantiated will not be penalized.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy consistently. Enforcement decisions do not give a contributor a right to merge; all changes remain subject to the review and governance rules in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).
